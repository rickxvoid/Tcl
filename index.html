Backlight Controller Bits Explained: The bit-depth of the backlight controller determines how many distinct dimming steps each zone can produce. QD-Mini LED's 16-bit controller offers 65,536 steps per zone. SQD Mini-LED's 26-bit controller delivers over 67 million steps — meaning far smoother gradients from pure black to peak brightness, eliminating visible banding and crush artifacts. RGB Mini-LED takes this further with a 30-bit controller (1 billion steps), the highest precision available in any consumer display.

TSR AI Processor: Exclusive to SQD and RGB Mini-LED models, the TSR (Temporal Super Resolution) AI chip analyzes each frame in real time, intelligently mapping backlight zones, enhancing upscaling, reducing motion blur, and applying per-scene HDR tone mapping that static algorithms cannot match.

BT.2020 vs DCI-P3: BT.2020 is the next-generation color standard that covers roughly 75% of all visible human color perception — significantly wider than DCI-P3 (~69%). Standard QLED and QD-Mini LED panels cover only ~97% of the smaller DCI-P3 space. SQD and RGB Mini-LED achieve 100% of the larger BT.2020 standard, unlocking colors that simply cannot be reproduced on previous-generation displays.
